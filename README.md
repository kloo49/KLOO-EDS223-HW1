# EDS223-HW1
Homework Assignment 1 - Map making practice
[Homework link](https://eds-223-geospatial.github.io/assignments/HW1.html)

This assignment uses data from the United States Environmental Protection Agency's previous EJSCreen: Environmental Justice Screening and Mapping Tool to identify environmental injustices.

The deliverables include two maps that communicate an environmental justice issue, and a brief paragraph to accompany the map. I focused on the relationship between cancer risk due to air toxics and proximity to traffic in San Diego and Orange County.
