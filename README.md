# EDS223 HW1 - Map Making Practise
[Assignment link](https://eds-223-geospatial.github.io/assignments/HW1.html)

This assignment uses data from the United States Environmental Protection Agency's previous EJScreen: Environmental Justice (EJ) Screening and Mapping Tool to identify environmental injustices. This dataset was published in 2023, with a 5-year summary from 2017 to 2021. I did not use the EJ and supplemental indexes as it combines environmental and socioeconomic indicators, and I wanted to explore the unprocessed data.

The deliverables include two maps that communicate an environmental justice issue, and a brief paragraph to accompany the map. I focused on the relationship between cancer risk due to air toxics and proximity to traffic in San Diego and Orange County as I have previously lived in these two areas, and would like to understand underlying issues that are more noticible at a larger scale.
